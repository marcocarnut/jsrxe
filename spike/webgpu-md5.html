WebGPU MD5 keycrack spike — [a-z]{n}

One GPU lane per candidate (grid-stride): unrank the index into an n-letter lowercase string, MD5 it in WGSL, binary-search the pasted digests. The high letters are enumerated on the host, the low ≤6 swept on the GPU (26⁶ < 2³²) — all 32-bit, no 64-bit emulation.

Default is md5("hello"). Add md5 of any 2–8 lowercase-letter word to have something to find.
